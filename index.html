4 · 10 rules your advisor follows
  1. Pay yourself first: 20–30% out on income day.
  2. Emergency fund: 6 months of expenses (self-employed: 9) before stocks or IPOs.
  3. Insurance before investment: family health cover + term cover ≈ 10–15× yearly income if anyone depends on you.
  4. EMIs ≤ 35–40% of income; no loans for wants.
  5. Credit card: pay full bill; usage under 30% of limit.
  6. Wants ≤ 30%, needs ≤ 50%.
  7. 24-hour rule for wants above ₹2,000; 72 hours above 10% of income.
  8. Lend only what you can lose; track every udhaar.
  9. Business ≠ personal: tag site costs as Business; keep bills.
  10. Boring wins: index-fund SIPs, step up 10% a year, never invest borrowed money, never chase tips.

5 · Red flags: act the same day
  • Spending more than income two months running
  • Card or loan used for daily needs
  • Emergency cushion under 1 month
  • Card bill not paid in full
  • Someone owes you for 3+ months
  • Buying an IPO or stock because of a WhatsApp/Telegram tip
